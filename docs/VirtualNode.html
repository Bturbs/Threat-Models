threat analysis

STRIDE

HoldingID:

  • Spoofing: yes (VNode1.HoldingID.1)
  • Tampering: no
  • Repudiation: yes (same as spoofing), final user can claim another x500 actor had the same HoldingID
  • Info disclosure: no (TBC HoldingID is not a secret/sensitive data)
  • DoS: no
  • ElevationOfPrivileges: yes, due to Spoofing

threatType

Spoofing (caused by HoldingID collision)

description

The entropy chosen for HoldingID is not enough to avoid collisions based on a controllable part of the attacker (any malleability/parameter of a X.500 name).The cost of this attack may make it feasible: For example In August 2022, the bitcoin block number 750884 has the SHA-256 HASH:000000000000000000001ad6481a4b43b110bea93cce4150e2779e3ce23db0e8. IT has 20 trailing 0 and then a '1' chars (HEX) it means 43 bits. The combined costs of generating a single collision with efficient ASIC hardware could be estimated to be less than a current Bitcoin clock reward 6.15 (TBC), the additional 5 bits of collision would cost less than 32 time 6.15 = 36.9 BTC that is less than 1 million USD as today countervalue. A successful attack may be economically sustainable. Also, the past cost of hashing has been decreasing and the cost may only be even lower in the future.
